Chromavox

Optical synthesizer: audio in modulates left-wall light emitters; rays cross the bench through your placed prisms / lenses / mirrors / glass; what reaches the right wall drives an additive synth out.

Mouse & touch

  • Tool button — drop element at bench center.
  • Click element to select; drag to move.
  • Shift-drag or right-button drag — rotate.
  • Two-finger pinch (touch) — scale + rotate.
  • Click left-wall tick — toggle that source on/off.
  • Shift-click or long-press tick — solo.

Keyboard

  • Arrows — move selected element ±5
  • Shift + ←/→ — rotate ±1°
  • Shift + ↑/↓ — resize ±8
  • Backspace / Delete — delete selected
  • Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z — undo / redo
  • Q — toggle audio out
  • H or ? — this help

Keyboard claviature (Audio in > Source = keyboard)

Z S X D C V G B H N J M — one octave of piano keys. , / . shift octave down / up.
